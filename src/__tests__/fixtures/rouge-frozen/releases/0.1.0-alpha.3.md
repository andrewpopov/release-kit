# Angel Snack 0.1.0-alpha.3 Patch Notes

Release date: 2026-07-06
Stage: alpha
Package version: 0.1.0-alpha.3

These notes are gathered from the release fragments in `docs/patch-notes/unreleased/`.

## Gameplay

- **Full charm pouches now pause the reward claim instead of losing the charm, so you can drop one and retry:** Internal groundwork only (ships dark — no visible UI change yet): when the charm pouch slot budget is enabled, claiming a charm reward with a full pouch no longer errors or silently drops the charm. The reward now waits so a future update can let you drop a charm and retry, or skip the reward outright.
- **Charm pouch slot budget groundwork (ships dark behind a debug flag):** Internal groundwork only (ships dark behind `debugMode.charmPouchBudget`): runs can now track a charm-pouch slot budget, with small charms and grand charms consuming different amounts of space. The default production experience is unchanged until the flag is enabled, but the saved-run and reward plumbing is in place for the upcoming pouch-capacity UI.
- **Charm Pouch overlay: view your slot budget, discard charms, and make room for a reward that doesn't fit:** Internal groundwork only (ships dark — behind the same debug flag as the pouch slot budget): adds the Charm Pouch overlay UI, reachable from combat, reward, and town screens. It shows your slot capacity as pips, lets you discard a charm (locked during combat, since bonuses are baked at fight start), and — when a reward charm won't fit — opens straight into a "make room" view where you can free a slot and take it, or decline the reward outright.
- **Refreshing the page mid-combat now resumes your fight instead of dropping you to the world map:** Reloading the game during a fight used to discard the encounter and drop you back on the world map. Your combat is now saved and restored, so a refresh (or coming back later) resumes the fight exactly where you left off. If a saved combat is too old to restore safely, we keep your run and return you to the world map instead of losing the run entirely.

## Balance

- **Charm focus pass — grand charms now center on one build-defining effect, themes spread out so a class's grands feel distinct, plus targeted dedup:** Grand charms got a focus pass. Each one now centers on a single build-defining effect with at most a couple of reinforcing riders, instead of a pile of unrelated bonuses — so equipping a grand tells you what your run is about. Their themes were also spread out so a class's grands pull toward different playstyles (execute, hard crowd-control, summon-army, traps, curses, guard-walls, energy economy, lifesteal, damage-over-time) rather than several variations of the same "every few cards, hit everything" effect. **Grand Charm: Eldritch Hunger** (Warlock) no longer overlaps Chaos Debt. It now leans into the void: each card you exhaust drains 4 magic damage from the target and heals you 3, and the first Exhaust each turn still draws a card and banks Energy — a single-target sustain engine. **Small Charm of Drumbeat** now grants +2 Mercenary Attack and +1 Damage (previously a duplicate +2 Damage). **Small Charm of Arcane Battery** now grants +1 Spell Damage and +5% Crit (previously a duplicate +2 Spell Damage).
- **Blacksmith Temper (-1 Energy) and Hone (+Draw 1) upgrades are now much pricier (240g / 200g) — premium mods are a rare splurge, not a routine buy.:** The Blacksmith's two strongest card upgrades are now premium purchases: **Temper** (card costs 1 less Energy) rises to 240 gold and **Hone** (card gains Draw 1) to 200 gold. Energy and card draw are the game's most powerful levers, so these are meant to be a rare, deliberate splurge rather than something you buy on every card. The mild stat upgrades — **Sharpen** (+3 damage) and **Fortify** (+3 Guard) — stay cheap at 50 gold.
- **Removed the Blacksmith's Refine service; card upgrades (card+) now come only from skill-tree ranks and the miniboss Forge Scroll, not gold.:** The Blacksmith no longer offers **Refine**, the option that bought a card's upgraded (card+) form for gold. Card upgrades now come from the two intended channels only: investing 5 ranks in a skill, or claiming a Forge Scroll from a miniboss reward. This keeps your skill trees the meaningful path to stronger cards rather than something gold can shortcut. The Blacksmith still offers card Evolutions and generic stat Upgrades.
- **The 8 class-signature attribute charms now cost 1 charm slot instead of 2:** The class-signature charms that grant a permanent attribute (Blood Oath, Aegis Pledge, Huntress Mark, Silent Strike, Nature's Blessing, Bone Pact, Arcane Keystone, Demon Pact) are small charms and now cost a single charm slot, matching every other small. They previously charged 2 slots — a leftover from before they were re-tiered — which only mattered once the charm-slot budget is in play.

## Content

- **18 new charms fill the freeze, bow, trap, curse, and defensive gaps — earned from existing achievements:** Eighteen new charms join the pool, closing coverage gaps and opening play patterns the roster was thin on: **Freeze / crowd-control**: Coldiron (pierce status resistance), Winterlock (frost when you're hit), Stillfrost (freeze when you end a turn untouched), and the barbarian grand Earthshaker (an opening stun). **Bow / multi-hit**: Fletching, Volley (a three-arrow salvo), and the amazon grand Strafe (auto-aims the weakest enemy). **Traps / delayed payoff**: Slow Fuse (a turn-4 timebomb), the Hourglass (refills Energy every 10 cards), and the assassin grand Tripwire (trap tempo + control). **Curses**: Afflictor's Edge (each status you land adds damage) and the necromancer grand Soulleech (afflictions heal you). **Defensive**: Stoneskin (flat damage reduction), Rampart (keep some Guard between turns), Lifeline (a once-per-run emergency heal), and the paladin grand Crown of Thorns (ramping retaliation). Plus the warlock grand Blood Ledger (pay Life for cards) and Wayfarer's Purse (gold + cheaper shops). Each is unlocked from an existing achievement track; the six class grands come from clearing all bosses with that class. Icons for the new charms are coming in a later update.

## UI

- **Add an admin feedback inbox for submitted bugs and suggestions.:** Admins now have a Bugs & Feedback tab that lists player-submitted bug reports and suggestions newest-first, including contact details and captured run context. This makes in-game feedback actionable after submission instead of disappearing into raw server storage or Discord-only alerts.
- **Full-screen character and run-end surfaces now share the canvas-fit height contract.:** Hero selection, expedition setup, and other full-screen cinematic surfaces now use the same canvas-fit height contract, removing the short bottom gap that appeared on the character flow.
- **Keep feedback form input actions covered by the UI dispatcher audit.:** Feedback form typing now uses registered input-dispatcher actions covered by the UI action audit. The visible form behaves the same, but future input refactors are less likely to silently break message/contact entry or force unnecessary full-screen rerenders while the player is writing a report.
- **Add in-game bug report and suggestion submissions with Discord bot notifications.:** Added a feedback option to the game menu so players can submit bug reports or suggestions without leaving the app.
- **Restyled the Terms and Privacy pages with richer legal-page backdrops and bordered document panels.:** The Terms and Privacy pages now use darker scene backdrops, stronger document-panel borders, and clearer legal navigation so the alpha service pages feel more like the rest of Angel Snack.
- **Adds a carved stone door backdrop outside the game frame.:** The letterbox space around the game now uses generated carved-stone door art, giving the app shell a darker dungeon-threshold frame instead of a generic warm wash.
- **Adds thematic art backdrops to previously generic screen surfaces.:** Combat Puzzles, puzzle results, Training, and Sim Lab now use curated thematic art layers instead of plain shell gradients, so those secondary screens feel anchored in the same carved-door, gothic campaign presentation as the rest of the game.

## Fixes

- **Repair review hygiene and boss-readiness scoring:** Cleaned up review-blocking lint/type issues and corrected boss-readiness scoring so balance reports judge boss pressure from the intended score terms. This is mostly behind-the-scenes, but it makes the generated balance guidance more trustworthy before tuning changes reach players.
- **Exploration charm rewards gracefully decline instead of stalling when the charm pouch is full (flag-gated):** When the charm pouch slot budget is enabled, finding a charm during exploration while your pouch is full no longer gets stuck on a dead choice — the charm is declined and the encounter continues normally. This system is currently flag-off in production.

## Tech

- **Added a cross-domain boundary ratchet that freezes private-namespace reach-through (no manual-play change):** Internal architecture guardrail (ROG-989). Documented the enforceable module boundary — a domain's `__ROUGE_*` namespaces are private, and its public read surfaces are the blessed cross-domain entry — and added a pretest ratchet that counts how often UI/sim code reaches into another domain's private internals and fails the build if that number grows. Existing coupling is frozen, not rewritten, so it can only shrink over time. No gameplay impact.
- **Parity-guard several hand-mirrored tables/sets so silent drift between duplicate copies fails the build:** Internal maintainability (ROG-943 drift-sweep). Several constants exist as hand-mirrored copies across the sim, UI, and runtime layers with only weak (or no) tests, so the copies could silently diverge. Added guards so drift fails the build: full-table equality for the runtime vs canonical `PROFICIENCY_SCALING_ATTRIBUTE` (ROG-979); an intersection parity guard between the player-facing armed-glow counter thresholds and the sim threshold specs (ROG-977); and structural invariants plus provenance breadcrumbs for the arena recommended-level-by-act table vs the XP schedule (ROG-978). No player-facing change.
- **Internal maintainability pass — single-sourced duplicated constants and hardened combat-save restore (no manual-play change):** Maintainability hardening (ROG-943/980). Several values that were hand-copied across files are now defined once and shared (joint-status counter IDs, the Barbarian discipline stances, the combat-log tone set, the fnv1a32 seed hash, and the XP curve), so they can no longer silently drift apart. Achievement-unlock evaluation used during profile migration was aligned to the canonical rule, and resumed combats now backfill any fields that a save predates, so older saves restore cleanly. These are internal correctness/robustness changes; normal play is unaffected.
- **Server ignores + self-removes a stale read-only maintenance flag so a crashed deploy can't wedge the host:** Internal ops hardening (ROG-1006). The server's read-only maintenance guard now ignores and self-removes a `maintenance.flag` older than 30 minutes (configurable via `ROUGE_MAINTENANCE_MAX_AGE_MS`). Previously a flag left behind by a crashed or killed deploy would keep every write returning 503 on that host until someone manually cleared it. The threshold stays well beyond the real deploy read-only window (~1–3 min) so it never fires mid-deploy. No player-facing change.
- **Autoplay/sim now value a mercenary's aura and behavior fit when hiring (no manual-play change):** The in-game autoplay and the balance sim now judge a mercenary contract by how well its aura and behavior fit the current deck, not by raw attack and life alone — so a merc whose aura is live for your build is preferred over one whose aura is dead for it. Manual play is unaffected.
- **Faster autoplay/sim decisions: memoized card-tree and base-card-id lookups (no gameplay change):** Internal performance pass for autoplay and simulation: repeated card-tree and base-card-id lookups are cached and reused during decision scoring. Gameplay decisions remain the same, but larger sim and planner runs spend less time recomputing static card metadata.
- **Sim diagnostic to measure whether the pilot needs foresight terms for enemy traits (no gameplay change):** Adds an internal balance-sim diagnostic (`trait-injection-ab`) that measures, per enemy trait, whether giving the auto-pilot a foresight term for it would change its decisions — via per-decision divergence plus a positive control — so we only add pilot logic where it demonstrably matters. All new scoring terms are diagnostic-only and off by default; manual and normal auto-play are unchanged.

## Operations

- **Deploy Discord announcements:** Deploys now attempt a once-per-version Discord patch-note announcement after the live server passes health checks, with a retry-safe ledger to avoid duplicate posts.
- **Add a dry-run workspace cleanup helper for local clutter.:** Maintainers can now dry-run and apply safe local workspace cleanup for ignored scratch folders, empty agent worktree leftovers, and the stray `:memory:` SQLite file without hand-written deletion commands.
